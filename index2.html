Our Story

A kitchen built around one long table

Kusina Isla started in 2016 as a weekend grill stand on a Cabuyao side street. Today it's a full dining room, but the idea hasn't changed: cook what came off the boat this morning, share it family-style, and let the fire do most of the talking.
